Internal Research Notes - Applied ML Team

Experiment 14: Transformer variants for long documents
We compared a standard Transformer, Longformer and a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) Transformer on our 8k-token contract summarisation dataset.
Longformer's sliding-window attention reduced memory use by 45 percent compared to full attention, with only a 1.2 point drop in ROUGE-L.
The MoE variant (8 experts, top-2 routing) gave the best ROUGE-L score (+2.1 over baseline) but training was unstable without a load-balancing loss.
Conclusion: use Longformer for cost-sensitive workloads and MoE when quality matters most.

Experiment 15: Agent designs
We tested three agent designs on 200 multi-hop internal questions:
1. Single-shot RAG (retrieve once, answer once): 61 percent accuracy.
2. ReAct agent with a single retriever tool: 74 percent accuracy.
3. ReAct agent with separate tools per knowledge source (tech docs, research notes, Wikipedia, ArXiv): 82 percent accuracy.
Giving each knowledge source its own clearly described tool helped the LLM pick the right source and reduced irrelevant context.

Experiment 16: Reflection step
Adding a self-reflection step, where the agent checks whether its draft answer is supported by the retrieved context, cut hallucinations by 30 percent but increased latency by 1.8 seconds on average.
